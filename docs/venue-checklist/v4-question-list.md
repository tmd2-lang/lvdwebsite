# Venue checklist v4: question list for approval

**Goal:** make it less professional and more useful without making it longer. The most technical questions move to short "For your planner" notes. Irene's 7 bride-tested questions go in. Everything is reworded in plain English.

**Result:** 45 questions become **42** (56 in the multicultural edition). There are **10 starred dealbreakers**, and they also go on one page near the front: "10 questions to never leave a tour without".

Key: **Keep** = same question, plainer wording. **New** = from the Reddit research and Irene's picks. **Merged** = two or more old questions become one. **Moved** = goes into that section's "For your planner" note. ★ = dealbreaker.

## 01 Date, Hours & Curfew (4 → 5)

| | v4 question | Was |
|---|---|---|
| Keep | What time can our vendors start setting up, and when does setup have to be finished? | What time may vendors begin load-in, and when must setup be complete? |

## The 10 starred dealbreakers (new front page)

**"10 questions to never leave a tour without"**

1. What time does everything have to end?
2. How many guests can the room really seat with your layout?
3. Can we see the rain-backup space, and does it fit everyone?
4. Do we have to use the venue’s caterer?
5. How many hours do we get to set up décor?
6. Can large installations get into the room?
7. Can florals, chandeliers, or draping hang from the ceiling?
8. Does the quote include everything?
9. Can our guest count go down after we sign, or only up?
10. What’s the deposit, and what happens if we cancel or postpone?

Each one gets a one-line "why it matters" under it in the full checklist. For example: *"Sign for 200, have 150 come, and you may still pay for 200."*

## Other pages

- **Red Flags:** cut from 8 lines to 4, sharing a page with Before You Sign:
  - "We're flexible" with nothing in writing
  - Only an hour or two to set up
  - The backup space doesn't fit everyone
  - The quote leaves out fees
- **Before You Sign:** cut from 5 steps to 3:
  - Get the full itemized quote
  - Have your designer check load-in and rigging
  - Make sure every approval is in the contract
- **Start Here** (Irene's 5 steps): kept, with the wording softened to "we recommend" where it reads like a rule.
- **Not added** (from the research, by Irene's choice): a medical emergency plan, timeline rules, and seeing the included items in person. The last one is partly covered by the new "Can we see the included pieces" in section 05.

Expected length: **12 pages** standard (14 multicultural), down from 13 and 15.
