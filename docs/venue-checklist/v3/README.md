# Venue checklist v3 (simpler version)

Built from Irene's V2.3 and Muse's cut list. Source: `index.html` (add `?edition=multicultural` for the multicultural edition).

## What changed

- **Questions:** 70 down to 45 in the standard edition. The multicultural edition has the same 45 plus a 14-question chapter (59 total).
- **Start Here page:** Irene's five steps from V2.3, with the "Your starting point" fill-in box.
- **Boxes:** one larger box per question, not two. Empty = not asked, slash = told out loud, check = confirmed in writing.
- **Stars:** 9 dealbreakers in the main sections (11 in multicultural). Only starred questions get Answer / Who said it lines.
- **Verdict line** at the end of each section: Clear / Needs follow-up / Dealbreaker.
- **Chase List page:** copy the slash items there and email them to the venue.
- **Designer handles the technical side:** freight, rigging, power and measuring are now a note, not tour questions.
- **Removed:** contents page.
- The standard edition is fully generic. All cultural questions (cultural caterers, cooking equipment, specific diets, processions, cultural structures, multiple ceremonies) live only in the multicultural chapter.

## Cut or simplified

**Section 05:** dance floor wrap is now just "May the dance floor be wrapped or covered?"

**Section 06, cut:** freight elevator specs, dock times, truck parking, union labor, carts/lifts, load-in fees, storage, final walkthrough, access delays.
**Section 06, simplified:** "Can large installations get into the room?" · "Is load-in easy for large pieces, or are there stairs and tight turns?" · "What time must the room be guest-ready?" · candles shortened.

**Section 07:** circuits/generator, rigging detail, cold-spark approvals and dry-ice rules cut. Kept as yes/no: ceiling hanging, cold sparks, cloud effect, haze, backup power.

**Section 08, cut:** garage limits, separate vendor parking, valet detail (now "required or optional, and what is the fee?"). Procession question moved to the multicultural chapter.

**Section 09:** elevators and docks removed from the total-cost list; vendor insurance certificates cut.

**What to bring:** tape measure replaced with phone photos and video.
**Before You Sign:** the designer walks and measures the load-in path, not the couple.
**Red flag:** freight measurements replaced with "No one can tell you if large installations can get in and be set up in time."

## Starred dealbreakers

Curfew · realistic capacity · weather backup · exclusive/outside catering · setup hours · large installations · ceiling hanging · full quote · deposit and cancellation. Multicultural adds: multiple ceremonies · sacred fire.
