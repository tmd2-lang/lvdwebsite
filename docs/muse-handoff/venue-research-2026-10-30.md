# What brides say about venue searching: October 30, 2026

For Irene and Muse. We read 10 Reddit threads (r/weddingplanning and r/BigBudgetBrides) where brides ask how to find a venue and what to ask on tours, and where brides who've been through it answer. This is for ad ideas and for the venue checklist. **Update, later on Oct 30:** Irene picked from these, and checklist v4 is now live (43 questions). See `../venue-checklist/v4/README.md` and `../venue-checklist/v4-question-list.md`. The "possible additions" section below is kept as the original proposal.

## Short version

- **Before booking, brides think the problem is *finding* a venue.** They don't know where to look, and they feel overwhelmed at every budget, from $10K to $500K.
- **After booking, brides say the problem was *vetting* it.** Their regrets are almost never about how the venue looked. They're about money surprises, rules that broke their plans, the rain plan, and who was actually in charge.
- **Brides want a ready-made list of questions.** Even brides who already have a planner ask Reddit for one. Answers are scattered across long comments, and people trade Google Sheets by DM. The checklist is the organized version of what they're looking for.

## What they feel before the tour

1. **Overwhelmed and unsure where to start.** Almost every original post uses the word.
2. **They only think of the fun questions.** One bride touring her dream venue could only think of questions about the s'mores bar.
3. **They're afraid the beauty will distract them** from asking the right things.
4. **They don't trust the listings.** Several say the big wedding sites are pay-to-play, so they dig for tagged photos and Google reviews.
5. **They're afraid the photos lie.** They want to see real weddings in the space, not styled shoots.
6. **Prices make them angry:** no price transparency and sticker shock. One bride: about $90K for venue, food and drink came to about $170K all-in.

## What they wish they'd asked (after)

- **Money:** what's *not* included (the most common regret), 18–22% service charges, surprise 3–4% admin fees, credit card fees, bartenders billed hourly, security, a $500–$1,000 DJ lighting add-on, "blank slate" venues that get expensive once you rent everything.
- **The guest count trap:** many contracts let you raise the count but not lower it. Sign for 200, 150 come, you still pay for 200.
- **The tour vs. the real day:** a gorgeous new bridal suite but the groom's "room" was a bathroom. "Included" glassware turned out to be plastic. The venue was decorated for the tour but none of it was included.
- **Rules that broke the plan:** floating candles only, no speeches during dinner, no hair and makeup on site (common at historic venues), music curfews, nothing hung from the ceiling.
- **Rain:** one bride's venue had no real rain plan. Brides say to make sure you'd also love the backup space.
- **Who's in charge:** passed between different venue staff, then a stranger ran the wedding day. A guest's medical emergency where venue staff didn't know what to do. (In another wedding, the planners handled one so smoothly the bride heard about it hours later.)
- **Practical:** not enough restrooms, cocktail hour space too small, the room flip, access times, clearing out the same night, accessibility for older guests.
- A venue sales manager in one thread said everything about services should be **confirmed in the contract**, which matches the checklist's "get it in writing" rule.

## Checklist: possible additions (Irene decides)

v3 already covers "what's included," fees in the quote, the rain backup space, backup power and outside caterers. These came up often in the threads and **don't seem to be in v3**:

1. Is there a **food and beverage minimum**? Is it lower on off-peak days?
2. Can we **lower the guest count** after signing, or only raise it?
3. Are there **admin or credit card fees** on top of the service charge?
4. How many **restrooms** are there for our guest count?
5. Is there a **getting-ready space** for both sides? Can hair and makeup work on site?
6. Will **another wedding or event** be on the property the same day or weekend?
7. Will the **same person** handle our planning and be there on the day?
8. What's the plan for a **medical emergency**?
9. Are there **rules about the timeline**, such as no speeches during dinner?
10. Can we **see the included items** in person (chairs, glassware, linens)?

Irene cut v3 down to 45 questions on purpose. If she wants any of these, they could replace weaker questions rather than add length.

## Ad angles this suggests

| Angle | Headline idea |
|---|---|
| The regret list | "Brides who've done it wish they'd asked these." |
| The guest count trap | "Sign for 200. 150 come. You pay for 200." |
| What's not included | "The tour shows you what's there. Ask what isn't." |
| Beauty blindness | "Fall in love on the tour. Read the fine print after." |
| Tour vs. reality | "The bridal suite" vs. "The groom's room" |
| The rain plan | "Would you still love the rain backup space?" |
| The planner (retargeting / Email 3) | "The best wedding days are the ones where you never hear about the problem." |

If the ads promise "what brides wish they'd asked," the checklist should actually answer those. That's one reason to consider the additions above.
