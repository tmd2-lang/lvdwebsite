Venue Checklist Emails

For Muse and Irene · October 29, 2026 · Live on the website

Short version. When a couple downloads the free venue checklist at ladyvictoriadesigns.com/venue-checklist, they now get three emails from Irene: one right away, one on day 3, and one on day 7. The website sends them automatically. Each email is personalized with her first name, and a few lines change depending on two answers she gave on the form. This page shows every version exactly as it will look.

Who gets which version

Still looking for a venueVenue already booked
Standard editionVersion AVersion B
Multicultural editionVersion CVersion D
  • Multicultural couples get the multicultural PDF, their own subject lines for Emails 1 and 3, a fourth question in Email 2 about naming their traditions, and an extra line in Email 3.
  • Booked couples get wording about confirming details with the venue they chose, instead of touring.
  • Everything else is the same for everyone.

How it works

  • Sent from “Irene at Lady Victoria Designs” through the studio’s website email account. Replies come back to that account.
  • Email 3’s button opens the Design Consultation booking page with her name and email filled in, and links the booking back to her record.
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link. If she unsubscribes, the remaining emails stop.
  • Each signup also appears in the admin inquiries list as “Venue checklist,” with her budget, wedding date, and venue status.

What we need from Irene

  • Approve the copy below, or tell us what to change.
  • Is the 20-minute consultation complimentary? If yes, Email 3 should say so. It is a strong reason to book.
  • Which inbox should replies go to? Email 1 asks couples to hit reply, so someone should be reading it.
  • “See how we plan” in Email 2: should it open the Planning page (current) or the inquiry form?

Email 2 · Sent on day 3

Standard edition · still looking

Subject: The three questions couples forget to ask

Lady Victoria Designs

Hi Valentina,

Most couples ask about capacity and catering. Far fewer ask about the hours before guests arrive, and that’s where beautiful plans quietly fall apart.

Three to add to your next tour:

  1. How many hours do we get to set up? If the answer is two, the design you’re picturing may not fit.
  2. Can large installations get into the room? Ask how they get in, not just whether. “It should be fine” is not an answer.
  3. What’s the true total? Service charges, tax, security, and overtime are rarely in the first quote.

These are the questions our planning team asks on every tour. If you’d rather we handled them for you:
