# Venue checklist update for Irene and Muse: October 30, 2026

What changed on the venue checklist since the Oct 29 launch, what's live, and what's next. The short version is also the first entry in the Log in `notes.md`.

## 1. The PDF is simpler (v3). Live.

Irene said v2 was still too busy, so v3 was rebuilt from her own V2.3 and Muse's cut list.

- **Fewer, shorter questions:** 70 down to **45** in the standard edition. The multicultural edition has the same 45 plus a **14-question** chapter (59 total).
- **Technical questions removed:** freight elevator specs, dock times, union labor, lifts, rigging detail, generators, dry-ice rules and vendor insurance. A line in the décor section now says the designer handles that side.
- **Standard edition is fully general.** Every cultural question (cultural caterers, cooking equipment, specific diets, processions, cultural structures, multiple ceremonies) is only in the multicultural chapter.
- **Irene's "Start Here" page** (from her V2.3) comes right after her letter: her 5 steps, the "Your starting point" fill-in box, and "Tour only those, with this checklist in hand."
- **New box system:** one box per question instead of two.
  - Empty = not asked
  - Slash = told yes out loud
  - Check = confirmed in writing
  - The legend is explained once, on the how-to page.
- **9 starred dealbreakers** (11 in multicultural). Only these get "Answer" and "Who said it" lines.
- **A verdict at the end of every section:** Clear / Needs follow-up / Dealbreaker.
- **Chase List page:** copy the slash items there and email them to the venue.
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - "Bring a tape measure" is now "bring your phone for photos and video".
  - The designer measures the load-in path, not the couple.
  - The freight red flag now reads "No one can tell you if large installations can get in and be set up in time."
- **Removed:** the contents page.

Source and full change list: `docs/venue-checklist/v3/` (README.md).

## 5. Mobile version of the checklist: built and live (Oct 30)

Opened from "Open it on your phone" on the signup thank-you screen and in Email 1. Everything below is in version one, except the last bullet.

Most signups come from Meta ads on phones, where the PDF means pinching and zooming and the boxes can't be ticked. The plan is a phone version of the same checklist, with the PDF staying as the print and download option.

- **Tap the boxes** to cycle empty, slash and check, and type answers under the starred questions.
- **One checklist per venue,** so a couple touring three venues can compare them side by side.
- **A Chase List that builds itself** from the slashes, with a ready-to-send email to the venue.
- **Saved to her lead, through a private link** in Email 1 (no account or password). It works on any phone or browser and survives Instagram's built-in browser, which would wipe a phone-only save. It also keeps a copy on the phone so it works on tours with bad signal.
- **Same questions as the PDF,** read from one shared list, so a question change only happens once.
- **Later, optional:** Irene could see which venues a couple is touring and what they marked as a dealbreaker before the consultation. If we add this, the app will say plainly that answers are saved with Lady Victoria Designs.
